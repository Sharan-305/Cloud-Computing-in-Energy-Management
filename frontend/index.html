    How Tamil Nadu Tariff Estimation Works

    Electricity billing in Tamil Nadu follows progressive slab structures approved by the Tamil Nadu Electricity Regulatory Commission (TNERC).

    • 0 to 100 units: 100% Free under the Tamil Nadu Government Domestic Subsidy scheme.
    • 101 to 200 units: Subsidized concessional rate of ₹2.25/unit.
    • 201 to 400 units: Standard energy rate of ₹4.50/unit.
    • Above 400 units: Higher tier rates with fixed charges and 5% Electricity Duty.

    XGBoost Usage Pattern & Forecasting

    Instead of inventing electricity prices, our machine learning pipeline strictly forecasts kilowatt-hour (kWh) consumption.

    • Chronological feature engineering: Lag-1, Lag-2, Lag-7, and 7-day rolling statistics.
    • Pattern identification: Stable, Increasing, Seasonal, Peak-Heavy, or Irregular.
    • Statistical anomaly detection to alert unexpected spikes or outages.
    • Pipes forecasted kWh through the official Tamil Nadu Tariff Engine to yield future cost estimates in ₹ (INR).
