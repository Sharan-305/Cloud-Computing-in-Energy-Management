    Data Ingestion Guidelines

    Consistent Unit: All energy measurements must strictly be recorded in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

    Data Integrity: Simulated readings are clearly flagged in the database and audit trail. They are never misrepresented as real physical meter readings.

    TNERC Billing Window: Tamil Nadu domestic connections are billed on a bi-monthly (60-day) cycle. Maintaining consistent daily or weekly readings helps improve XGBoost model forecast accuracy.
