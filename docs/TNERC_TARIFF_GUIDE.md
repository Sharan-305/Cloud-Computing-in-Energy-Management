# Tamil Nadu Electricity Regulatory Commission (TNERC) Tariff Guide

## 1. Domestic Category (LT Tariff 1A) - Bi-Monthly Billing

In Tamil Nadu, residential electricity consumers are billed on a **bi-monthly** (every two months) cycle by TANGEDCO.

### Slabs and Free Units Scheme:
- **0 to 100 units:** **100% Free** under the Government of Tamil Nadu Free 100 Units Scheme (G.O.Ms.No.27, Energy Department). Gross charge of ₹4.50/unit is fully reimbursed by government subsidy, resulting in **₹0.00** payable and waived fixed charge.
- **101 to 200 units:** First 100 units free. Next 100 units billed at a subsidized rate of ₹2.25/unit (₹4.50 gross - ₹2.25 subsidy).
- **201 to 400 units:** 0-100 free; 101-200 at subsidized rate; 201-400 at ₹4.50/unit.
- **401 to 500 units:** Billed at ₹6.00/unit for units exceeding 400.
- **Above 500 units:** Higher progressive slabs:
  - 501 to 600 units: ₹8.00/unit
  - 601 to 800 units: ₹9.00/unit
  - 801 to 1000 units: ₹10.00/unit
  - Above 1000 units: ₹11.00/unit

### Fixed Charges & Duties:
- **Fixed Charge:** ₹30.00 to ₹50.00 per bi-monthly bill (waived for consumption ≤ 100 units).
- **Electricity Duty:** 5.0% on net energy charges.

---

## 2. Commercial Category (LT Tariff IIIA) - Monthly Billing

- 0 to 100 units: ₹6.50/unit
- 101 to 500 units: ₹8.50/unit
- Above 500 units: ₹10.00/unit
- Fixed Demand Charge: ₹120.00/month
- Electricity Duty: 5.0%

---

## 3. Industrial Category (LT Tariff IIIB) - Monthly Billing

- Standard Rate: ₹7.65/unit
- Fixed Demand Charge: ₹150.00/month
- Electricity Duty: 5.0%

---

## 4. Net Bill Calculation Formula

$$\text{Net Estimated Bill} = \text{Gross Energy Charge} - \text{Government Subsidy} + \text{Fixed Charge} + \text{Electricity Duty (5\%)} + \text{Adjustments}$$

All bills produced by the system are clearly labeled as **"Estimates"** and retain the tariff version `TNERC-TANGEDCO-2024-V1`.
